Productivity

10 habits for a more focused team

Agree on a small number of priorities, name owners, and protect time for focused work. Close the loop with short reviews so the plan stays useful when priorities shift.

AI

A practical guide to AI in project work

Use AI for a first pass at summaries, task breakdowns, or recurring updates. Keep people responsible for reviewing recommendations, checking sensitive details, and making decisions that affect the team.

Teamwork

Build a strong remote team culture

Make expectations visible, document decisions where the work happens, and leave room for synchronous and asynchronous collaboration. A predictable rhythm helps people contribute across time zones.

Leadership

Plan a project from idea to launch

Define the outcome first. Break the work into milestones, give each milestone an owner, surface dependencies early, and review progress often enough to act on changes.

Productivity

Choose signals that help teams improve

Track a small set of measures tied to delivery and capacity. Use them to start better conversations about bottlenecks and quality, not to rank individuals or replace judgment.

Teamwork

Make handoffs easier to follow

Capture the decision, owner, next step, and relevant links together. Clear handoffs reduce repeated questions while leaving room to ask for missing context.
